SHOPMATE SHIPPING POLICY

DOMESTIC SHIPPING (United States)

Standard Shipping
- Cost: FREE on orders over $50; $4.99 on smaller orders
- Delivery time: 3 to 5 business days from order confirmation
- Carrier: USPS Ground Advantage or UPS SurePost (carrier chosen by ShopMate)
- Tracking: Provided within 24 hours of label creation
- Coverage: All 50 US states including Alaska and Hawaii. Note that Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories may take 5 to 8 business days.

Expedited Shipping
- Cost: $14.99 flat
- Delivery time: 1 to 2 business days
- Carrier: UPS 2nd Day Air or FedEx Express Saver
- Cutoff: Orders placed before 2 PM Pacific Time ship the same business day

Overnight Shipping
- Cost: $29.99 flat
- Delivery time: Next business day
- Carrier: UPS Next Day Air or FedEx Priority Overnight
- Cutoff: Orders placed before 12 PM Pacific Time ship the same business day
- Not available for PO Boxes, APO/FPO, or US territories

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING

We currently ship to over 60 countries including Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates.

Standard International
- Cost: Calculated at checkout based on weight and destination, typically $19.99 to $49.99
- Delivery time: 7 to 14 business days
- Customs and duties: The customer is responsible for any import duties, taxes, or customs fees levied by the destination country.

Express International
- Cost: Calculated at checkout, typically $39.99 to $89.99
- Delivery time: 3 to 6 business days
- Carrier: DHL Express or FedEx International Priority

ORDER PROCESSING

- Orders are processed Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays.
- Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.
- Most orders ship within 1 business day of being placed.
- Large items such as the Zen Ergonomic Office Chair may take 2 to 3 business days to process due to additional packaging.

ADDRESS CHANGES

- Address changes must be requested within 1 hour of placing the order, before the order enters the picking queue.
- After 1 hour, address changes are not guaranteed but customer support will attempt to intercept.
- Orders cannot be redirected after they have shipped.

LOST OR DAMAGED PACKAGES

- If a package is marked delivered but you have not received it, please wait 48 hours then contact support@shopmate.example.
- Damaged packages must be reported within 7 days of delivery with photos. We will issue a replacement or refund.
- Lost packages are eligible for a free replacement after a 10-business-day investigation with the carrier.

NOT SHIPPING TODAY

We do not ship to: Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Russia, Belarus, Crimea, or any country sanctioned by the US Office of Foreign Assets Control.
