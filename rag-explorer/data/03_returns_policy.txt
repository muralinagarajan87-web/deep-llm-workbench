SHOPMATE RETURNS AND REFUNDS POLICY

RETURN WINDOW

You may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund of the item price. The 30-day window begins the day the carrier marks the package as delivered.

Holiday extension: Orders placed between November 1 and December 24 may be returned through January 31 of the following year.

ELIGIBILITY

To be eligible for a return, the item must be:
- In its original packaging including all accessories, manuals, and tags
- Unused and in resellable condition
- Free of physical damage caused by the customer

Items NOT eligible for return:
- Opened software, digital downloads, gift cards
- Personal-care items where seal has been broken (e.g., earbuds tips, cleaning products)
- Custom or personalized engravings
- Final-sale items (clearly marked at checkout)
- Cloud Memory Foam Pillow (SKU P010) once removed from sealed packaging, for hygiene reasons

HOW TO START A RETURN

1. Log into your ShopMate account and visit the Orders page.
2. Click "Start a return" next to the relevant item.
3. Select a reason and choose your refund method (original payment or store credit).
4. Print the prepaid return label we email you.
5. Drop the package at any UPS or USPS location within 14 days of label creation.

If you do not have an account (guest checkout), email support@shopmate.example with your order number.

REFUNDS

- Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method when possible.
- Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to a ShopMate error.
- Store credit refunds are issued instantly upon scan-in at the carrier.

EXCHANGES

We do not process direct exchanges. To exchange, return the original item for a refund and place a new order.

DEFECTIVE OR INCORRECT ITEMS

If you received a defective, damaged, or incorrect item:
- Contact support within 7 days of delivery
- We will provide a prepaid return label and a replacement at no cost
- For defective electronics, the manufacturer warranty may also apply (see Warranty Policy)

LARGE ITEMS

Furniture (e.g., Zen Ergonomic Office Chair) requires scheduled pickup by our logistics partner. A $40 pickup fee is deducted from the refund unless the return is due to a defect or our error.

REFUND TIMELINES BY PAYMENT METHOD

- Credit and debit cards: 5 to 7 business days after we process the refund
- PayPal: 1 to 3 business days
- Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay: 5 to 7 business days
- Store credit: Instant
- Buy-now-pay-later (Klarna, Afterpay): 7 to 10 business days

PRICE ADJUSTMENTS

If an item you purchased goes on sale within 14 days of your order, contact support and we will refund the difference as store credit.
