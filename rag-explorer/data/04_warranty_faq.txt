SHOPMATE WARRANTY FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What is the standard warranty period?
A: Most electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty starting from the delivery date. Wearables such as the Nimbus Smart Watch Series 5 carry a 2-year warranty. Furniture (Zen Ergonomic Office Chair) has a 5-year structural warranty. Apparel and accessories carry a 90-day defect warranty. The Trail Insulated Water Bottle has a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects.

Q: What does the warranty cover?
A: The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. This includes failures of internal components, premature battery degradation (less than 80% capacity within the warranty period for batteries), and structural defects.

Q: What is NOT covered?
A: The warranty does not cover:
- Accidental damage, drops, or liquid damage outside rated water resistance
- Cosmetic wear such as scratches and scuffs
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Loss or theft
- Normal wear and tear (cable fraying, fabric pilling, foam compression)
- Consumable parts such as ear-tips, keyboard switches replaced after warranty start, or pillow covers

Q: How do I file a warranty claim?
A: Email warranty@shopmate.example with your order number, a description of the issue, and photos or video showing the defect. Our team will respond within 2 business days. If approved, we will send a prepaid shipping label for the defective unit and ship a replacement upon receipt.

Q: Can I purchase an extended warranty?
A: Yes. Extended warranty plans (ShopMate Care+) are available at checkout for eligible products. Plans extend coverage by 1 or 2 additional years and add accidental damage protection (with deductible).
- 1-year extension: starts at $19.99
- 2-year extension with accidental damage: starts at $39.99

Q: Is the warranty transferable?
A: The standard manufacturer warranty is non-transferable and applies only to the original purchaser. ShopMate Care+ plans are likewise tied to the original purchaser and order.

Q: What is the battery replacement policy?
A: For products with non-replaceable batteries (e.g., Aurora Wireless Headphones, Nimbus Smart Watch), if the battery falls below 80% of original capacity within the warranty period, we will replace the unit at no cost. After the warranty period, paid battery service is available for $59 (headphones) or $89 (smartwatch).

Q: How long does a warranty replacement take?
A: After we receive the defective unit, replacement units typically ship within 3 business days. The replacement carries the remainder of the original warranty or 90 days, whichever is longer.

Q: Does the warranty cover items damaged by water?
A: Only damage that occurs within the product's rated water resistance is covered. For example, the Pulse Bluetooth Speaker is IPX7 (submersion up to 1m for 30 minutes). Damage from saltwater, chlorinated pools, or pressurized water (showers, dishwashers) is excluded for all products.

Q: What happens if my product is discontinued and needs warranty service?
A: We will replace it with the closest equivalent current model at no additional cost.
